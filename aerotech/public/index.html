LOCAL CONNECTION

Bring RESIDUAL onto the floor.

AeroTech reads your existing Command Station. Configure its address when launching AeroTech:

node server.cjs --station http://127.0.0.1:8765

Use the address your station actually runs on. AeroTech selects a free port for itself, so your other services can stay running.

Add --control to enable run, triage, pause and resume requests. Full controls stay in Command Station. Its session token remains in the local bridge.
